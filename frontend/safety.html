Clinical Safety

CardioX is a cardiovascular decision support platform concept designed to assist healthcare professionals with structured risk review, interpretable outputs, and safer presentation of AI-supported information. System Position

What CardioX is designed to do

CardioX is designed as a clinical decision support system, not as a diagnostic tool or autonomous medical authority. Its purpose is to assist healthcare professionals by presenting structured patient information, probability-based risk outputs, and supporting interpretation features in a clear and reviewable format.

The platform is intended to complement professional judgement rather than replace it. Decisions relating to diagnosis, treatment, escalation, and patient care must remain the responsibility of qualified clinicians working within appropriate healthcare governance.

Safety Principles

Core principles that guide safe use

HO

Human oversight

All CardioX outputs should be reviewed and interpreted by a clinician. The platform does not make autonomous clinical decisions and does not remove the need for professional judgement.

TR

Transparency

Risk outputs are presented using percentages, risk bands, and explainability-oriented features to support clearer understanding of how results should be reviewed in practice.

CP

Clarity of purpose

CardioX is positioned as a decision support platform concept rather than a certified medical device, helping reduce over-reliance, misuse, or inappropriate interpretation.

Model Boundaries

Important limitations of the prediction model

The machine learning model used in CardioX is trained on a UCI Cleveland-style heart disease dataset. While useful for structured model development and probability-based risk experimentation, this dataset does not fully capture the breadth of real-world patient populations, local service variation, or all forms of clinical complexity.

For that reason, outputs should never be interpreted in isolation. Predictions should always be considered alongside patient history, symptom context, additional tests, clinician expertise, and the wider clinical picture.

Data and Privacy

Data-related safety considerations

DU

Prototype data usage

CardioX is developed using publicly available structured datasets and simulated workflows rather than live hospital patient data within the current implementation.

DB

Data storage

The current platform uses SQLite as a lightweight persistence layer suited to the present system implementation and demonstration workflow.

GD

Privacy awareness

In a real deployment context, healthcare-grade data protection controls, privacy safeguards, and regulatory compliance requirements such as GDPR would be essential.

Ethical Design

Ethical considerations in AI-supported healthcare

BF

Bias and fairness

Machine learning models can reflect limitations or biases present in their training data. This must be considered carefully when reviewing outputs in a healthcare context.

RS

Responsibility

CardioX provides support, not authority. Clinicians remain responsible for professional review, decision-making, and all resulting care actions.

SC

Safe communication

Outputs are presented in a structured and cautious way to reduce the risk of misinterpretation, misplaced confidence, or overstatement of model capability.

Clinical Use Notice

Important notice

CardioX is intended to support cardiovascular risk review and professional clinical decision-making. It is not a certified medical device and should not be used as a replacement for clinician judgement, diagnosis, or treatment planning.

All outputs should be interpreted by qualified healthcare professionals and used only as one part of a broader clinical assessment process carried out within appropriate healthcare governance.
