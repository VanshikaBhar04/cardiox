Quick Start Workflow

CardioX is designed around a simple workflow so users can move efficiently from patient selection to structured assessment and review.

1

Open the main workspace

Go to the main dashboard and access the patient or user management area based on your role.

2

Find or create a patient

Search the record table for an existing patient or add a new patient if no match exists.

3

Select the record

Click a patient row to load their summary, edit form, and latest assessment history.

4

Begin assessment

Open the structured clinical assessment form and enter the required values.

5

Review outputs

Interpret the risk result, explainability, advice, and trend information together.

6

Export the report

Generate a clinician-facing or patient-facing PDF depending on the use case.

Assessment Form Guidance

The clinician assessment form captures structured cardiovascular inputs used by the model.

Age

Automatically calculated from the patient’s date of birth and used as a core cardiovascular risk factor.

Sex

Loaded from the patient record and used to reflect demographic differences in clinical risk patterns.

Chest Pain (cp)

Represents the patient’s chest pain presentation, including typical angina, atypical angina, non-anginal pain, or asymptomatic presentation.

Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps)

The patient’s resting systolic blood pressure. Higher values may contribute to increased cardiovascular risk.

Cholesterol (chol)

Total cholesterol input used by the model. Elevated cholesterol may contribute to increased cardiovascular risk.

Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs)

Indicates whether fasting blood sugar is greater than 120 mg/dL.

Rest ECG (restecg)

Represents the resting electrocardiographic result and may reflect normal or abnormal cardiac activity.

Max Heart Rate (thalch)

The maximum heart rate achieved during testing or clinical measurement.

Exercise-Induced Angina (exang)

Indicates whether angina is triggered during exertion, which can be clinically significant for interpretation.

Oldpeak

Represents ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest, often used in cardiovascular evaluation.

Slope

Describes the slope of peak exercise ST segment and contributes to the model’s interpretation.

CA and Thal

These structured clinical attributes provide additional model input for interpreting cardiovascular risk patterns.

How to Read the Results

CardioX is a decision-support prototype and should always be used alongside professional judgement.

Risk Percentage

A probability-based estimate generated from the patient’s structured input values.

Risk Band

Low, Moderate, or High groupings designed to make interpretation clearer and more practical.

SHAP Top Drivers

The most influential features that pushed the model result upward or downward for that individual case.

Personalised Advice

Model-informed, source-backed advice designed to support practical interpretation of the assessment.

Trend Visualisation

Shows how selected values compare over time against a reference line for easier review.

Clinical Judgement

The platform supports decision-making, but final interpretation must remain with the clinician or authorised user.
