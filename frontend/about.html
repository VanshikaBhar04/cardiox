About CardioX

Built to support clearer, safer, and more usable cardiovascular risk assessment.

CardioX is a clinician-facing cardiovascular decision support platform designed for hospitals, NHS settings, healthcare professionals, and cardiology teams. It combines structured patient assessment, probability-based risk prediction, interpretable outputs, and workflow-focused design to support more confident clinical review.

Our Positioning

What CardioX is

CardioX is designed as a modern digital health platform concept focused on making cardiovascular risk assessment more usable, transparent, and clinically practical.

CardioX has been created to show how machine learning can be integrated into a professional healthcare workflow through a clean web interface, structured patient assessment, and clear presentation of risk outputs. The platform is centred on usability, professional trust, and responsible clinical support rather than opaque or hard-to-interpret outputs.

Instead of positioning AI as a replacement for clinical expertise, CardioX is designed to support healthcare professionals by making risk information easier to interpret, review, document, and communicate. The overall aim is to provide a more practical digital experience for cardiovascular decision support in real clinical environments.

Why It Matters

Why CardioX was created

01

The challenge

Many traditional cardiovascular risk tools deliver a final score but provide limited visual clarity, limited workflow support, and limited insight into how results should be interpreted during day-to-day clinical use.

02

The response

CardioX was designed to present AI-supported cardiovascular risk assessment in a way that feels more operational, more transparent, and more aligned with the needs of healthcare professionals, cardiology teams, and structured hospital workflows.

How The Platform Works

A structured end-to-end assessment workflow

IN

Structured clinical input

Clinicians enter relevant cardiovascular attributes such as age, chest pain type, cholesterol, blood pressure, and other structured clinical factors used within the assessment workflow.

AI

Probability-based risk prediction

CardioX uses a machine learning model to estimate heart attack risk as a probability score, which is then presented as both a percentage and a risk band for faster and clearer review.

REV

Clinical review and reporting

The platform supports assessment history, trend review, personalised guidance, and report export so that outputs can be reviewed and documented in a more structured and practical way.

Technology Foundation

Dataset and model information

CardioX uses a UCI Cleveland-style heart disease dataset as the basis for model development and experimentation. This dataset includes structured cardiovascular variables commonly used in heart disease prediction research and supports the development of consistent probability-based risk assessment logic.

The deployed prediction model in the current system is a Random Forest classifier. This model was selected because it provides strong performance for probability-based risk prediction while supporting the wider goal of delivering a practical and clinically reviewable decision support experience.

Application workflow records and supporting data operations are managed using SQLite, providing lightweight persistence suitable for the current implementation while maintaining a clear pathway for future architecture expansion.

Clinical Use Notice

Important notice

CardioX is intended to support cardiovascular risk review and clinical decision-making. It is not designed to replace clinician judgement, diagnosis, or treatment planning, and should be used within appropriate professional governance and healthcare oversight.
